Call for Grand Challenge Proposals

ACM ICMR 2027 is calling for Grand Challenge (GC) proposals. The ICMR Grand Challenge track serves as a competitive arena that engages researchers and practitioners in tackling pressing, innovative, and real-world multimedia search and retrieval problems. Proposers are invited to organize challenges that push the boundaries of current state-of-the-art methodology, establish high-standard benchmarks, provide accessible datasets, and foster reproducibility and community engagement.

We particularly encourage Grand Challenge proposals aligned with the conference special themes:

  • Multimedia retrieval in embodied AI environments: episodic retrieval, multimodal memory indexing for embodied agents, retrieval-augmented embodied AI, interactive multimodal navigation, and robotics.
  • Cultural grounding and retrieval: cultural QA systems, low-resource cultural retrieval, and cultural and social norms in multimodal multilingual retrieval.

Proposal Requirements

Proposers with an innovative idea for a Grand Challenge are invited to submit a proposal (PDF, maximum 4 pages, ACM ICMR format) addressing the following key criteria:

  • State the Goals and Impact: Articulate the significance and relevance of the challenge problem for the multimedia retrieval community, industry, and society. Explain why existing solutions are insufficient and what breakthrough the challenge aims to stimulate.
  • Define the Core Research Tasks: Detail the specific research tasks or sub-tasks that participants will be required to address. Clearly formulate the problem inputs, expected outputs, and scope.
  • Contextualize Against the State-of-the-Art: Provide an overview of current state-of-the-art methods and explain how this Grand Challenge will advance the field beyond existing benchmarks.
  • Provide Accessible Datasets: Supply direct links to the datasets used for training and objective evaluation, alongside complete and accessible documentation, data split protocols, and licensing.
  • Specify Evaluation Metrics and Resource Limits: Formulate rigorous, objective evaluation criteria and procedures. This section must explicitly state the operational constraints for participants, specifying whether teams may leverage unlimited cloud compute infrastructure or must adhere to strict hardware and resource limits.
  • Deliver Task Specifications: Elaborate on the comprehensive breakdown of the challenge tasks, sub-tasks, evaluation timeline, baseline models (if available), and their corresponding datasets.
  • Organizing Committee: Include contact details, affiliations, and short biographies for at least two main organizers accountable for managing, promoting, reviewing, and evaluating submissions as outlined in the proposal.

Presentation and Publication

Organizers of accepted Grand Challenges will oversee the challenge execution and lead the following activities:

  • Challenge Overview Paper: Challenge organizers are expected to present an overview paper summarizing the GC motivation, design, dataset, evaluation methodologies, and a synthesis of the participating teams' results.
  • Participant Papers & Showcase: Challenge organizers will invite teams participating in the GC to submit challenge papers and present their solutions during ICMR 2027 in Singapore.
  • ACM Proceedings Publication: Both the GC overview paper and accepted participant papers will be published in the ACM ICMR proceedings. Each paper should not be longer than 4 pages (plus 2 additional pages for the list of references).

Proposal Submission Guidelines

Proposal Format

Each Grand Challenge proposal must be up to 4 pages (in PDF format) and formatted according to the ACM proceedings style. Click here to access LaTeX and Microsoft Word templates for this format. If you use LaTeX, please use sample-sigconf.tex as the template (or see the Overleaf template here).

How to Submit

Proposals should be submitted by email directly to the Grand Challenge Chairs at:

Please include "[ICMR 2027 Grand Challenge Proposal] <Challenge Title>" in the email subject line.
