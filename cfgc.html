Call for Grand Challenge Proposals

ACM ICMR 2027 is calling for grand challenge (GC) proposals. Proposers with an innovative idea of a grand challenge are invited to submit a proposal (PDF with max 4 pages, ACM ICMR paper format) following the requirements below.

Proposal Requirements

  • State the Goals and Impact: Articulate the significance and relevance of the challenge problem for the multimedia retrieval community, industry, and society.
  • Define the Core Research Tasks: Detail the specific research tasks or sub-tasks that participants will be required to address.
  • Contextualize Against the State-of-the-Art: Provide an overview of current state-of-the-art methods and explain how this Grand Challenge will advance the field beyond existing benchmarks.
  • Provide Accessible Datasets: Supply direct links to the datasets used for training and objective evaluation, alongside complete and accessible documentation.
  • Specify Evaluation Metrics and Resource Limits: Formulate rigorous, objective evaluation criteria and procedures. This section must explicitly state the operational constraints for participants, specifying whether teams may leverage unlimited cloud compute infrastructure or must adhere to strict hardware and resource limits.
  • Deliver Task Specifications: Elaborate on the comprehensive breakdown of the challenge tasks, sub-tasks, and their corresponding datasets.
  • Organizing Committee: Include contact details for at least two main organizers accountable for managing, promoting, reviewing, and evaluating submissions as outlined in the proposal.

Presentation and Publication

Proposers are expected to 1) present an overview paper to summarize the GC, and 2) invite teams participating in the GC to present their works in ICMR 2027. GC papers will be published in the ICMR proceedings. Each paper should not be longer than 4 pages (plus 2 additional pages for the list of references).

Proposal Submission

Proposals should be submitted by email to the GC Chairs at grand_challenge_chair@icmr2027.org.
